DBRepro is a joint project with Kingbase that synthesizes high-fidelity proxy databases to reproduce slow queries without exposing production data.

The system focuses on reproducing slow-query behavior through data generation and hybrid constraint solving. It has been validated on a real industrial workload with dozens of slow queries, average runtimes above 100 seconds, and a 636 GB production-scale scenario.
