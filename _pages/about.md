I am a PhD student in the [DEKE-DBSys](https://ruc-deke.github.io/dbsys/) group at Renmin University of China, where I work on database testing and workload generation. I am co-advised by Prof. [Xiaoyong Du](http://iir.ruc.edu.cn/~duyong/) and Prof. [Shuang Liu](https://liushuangcs.github.io/). I received my bachelor's degree in Computer Science and Technology from Shandong University in 2023.

My current research focuses on testing database systems and generating effective workloads for DBMS evaluation. You can reach me at `zhaoyangzhang@ruc.edu.cn`.
